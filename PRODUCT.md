# ODIN Pocket

## Register
product

## Users
Hackathon judges and developers who want to run and understand a compact local language model. They bring a short passage, explore a continuation, compare possible endings, and inspect the evidence behind the checkpoint.

## Product Purpose
Make a from-scratch model useful and inspectable on ordinary hardware. A complete interaction runs locally, displays actual model output and resource measurements, and identifies its limitations. Benchmark performance and reproducibility determine submission quality.

## Brand Personality
Precise, curious, composed. Assumed under the user's instruction to choose and build the product autonomously.

## Anti-references
Avoid chat interfaces implying instruction-following that the model has not learned; inflated benchmark counters; marketing pages that conceal the actual experiment; ornamental dashboards.

## Design Principles
The passage is the primary workspace. Every result has an observable source. Evidence is one click away. Unmeasured and failed results remain visible. All text processing stays local.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, readable text contrast, reduced motion, responsive layouts, labeled inputs, live status announcements, no color-only result encoding.
